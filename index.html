Design exploration

Three ways to lay out the long data forms

The onboarding and readiness forms are long. Here are three layout patterns to compare. Option A is built live in this prototype — open it to click through.

A · Sectioned sidebarLIVE

Jump between categories from a sticky sidebar; one section on screen at a time. Best for dense data with clear groups. Chosen for this build.

B · Step-by-step wizard

One question-group per step with Back/Next and a progress bar. Friendliest for first-timers, but slower to scan or revisit a single field.

C · One long page

Everything on one scrolling page, grouped by heading. Fast for power users and great for review, but daunting on first open.
